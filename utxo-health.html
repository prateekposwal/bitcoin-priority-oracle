Why It Matters

UTXO Health Is Network Health

Every UTXO must be stored by every full node on fast storage. Unlike OP_RETURN data (which can be pruned), UTXOs must be tracked forever. The SegWit witness discount made creating data-heavy UTXOs cheaper, but the storage cost is borne collectively.

Dust UTXOs — outputs worth less than the cost to spend them — bloat the set without providing economic utility. They increase node resource requirements and raise the barrier to running a full node.

Inscription UTXOs compound this: they carry large witness data that was never intended to be permanent state. Each inscription UTXO added today must be tracked for the life of the network.

The result: a growing storage externality that isn't priced into transaction fees. Miners collect fees for the congestion of including data — but the permanence cost is socialized across all node operators.

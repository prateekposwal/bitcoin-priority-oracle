Fee Sensitivity channel open cost vs fee rate

How the cost to open one channel changes with on-chain fee rates (using your parameters). Bars scale relative to the highest cost.

Lightning channels require on-chain transactions to open and close. Each channel open creates UTXOs that live in every full node's UTXO set. Your model estimates this externality at ~$0.0007/channel/year — negligible individually, but significant at scale. A Lightning node operator managing 100 channels over 3 years faces $0.21 in storage externalities alone, beyond the on-chain fees already paid. As channel counts grow, this externality becomes a real cost borne by the entire network.

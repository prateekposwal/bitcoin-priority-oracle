The Thesis

Congestion vs. Permanence

Every inscription adds data every full node must store forever. The miner who included it captured the fee — but the cost of storing that data for the next 20+ years is borne by every future node operator.

The SegWit 4× witness discount (BIP-141) was designed for malleability, not state economics. It creates an asymmetric incentive: inscribers pay a fraction of the true resource cost, while the network's storage burden grows unbounded.

This is the unpriced externality at the heart of Bitcoin's blockspace market. The fee market prices access to the next block — not the permanent state footprint of what goes into it.
